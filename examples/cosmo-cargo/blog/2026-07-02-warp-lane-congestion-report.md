Solar flare season pushed average transit times on the Sirius corridor up by 11% this quarter. Here
is what happened, and what we changed to keep your cargo moving.

## By the numbers

| Corridor         | Avg. transit | Change |
| ---------------- | ------------ | ------ |
| Sirius ↔ Sol     | 3.4 cycles   | +11%   |
| Vega ↔ Altair    | 1.9 cycles   | −2%    |
| Kepler ↔ Proxima | 5.1 cycles   | +4%    |

## Rerouting through the Vega bypass

From July, shipments booked on the Sirius corridor during flare warnings are rerouted through the
Vega bypass at no extra cost. The `route` field on shipment events now reports the lane actually
used.

## What's next

We are commissioning two additional relay beacons on the Kepler corridor, which should bring transit
times back to baseline by the end of Q3.
