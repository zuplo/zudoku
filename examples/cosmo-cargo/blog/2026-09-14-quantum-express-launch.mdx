After eighteen months of closed trials with 140 partner shipyards, Quantum Express is open to every
Cosmo Cargo account. Parcels now arrive within the same orbital cycle they were booked in, anywhere
in the Orion Arm.

![The Quantum Express hub at Station Alpha-7](/space.webp)

## What's new

Quantum Express splits each shipment into an entangled pair: one half stays at the origin hub, the
other materializes at the destination. Tracking reads the origin half, so status updates are instant
and never lost to relay lag.

- **Guaranteed arrival windows** of ±4 standard minutes
- **Live tracking** through the existing `GET /shipments/{id}/events` endpoint
- **No extra integration work** for accounts already on the Shipment API

## Booking a Quantum Express shipment

Set the service level when you create the shipment:

```ts title="create-shipment.ts"
const response = await fetch("https://api.cosmocargo.dev/shipments", {
  method: "POST",
  headers: { "Content-Type": "application/json" },
  body: JSON.stringify({
    origin: "STATION-ALPHA-7",
    destination: "KEPLER-442B-DOCK-3",
    serviceLevel: "quantum-express",
  }),
});
```

:::tip

Entangled pairs are capped at 2 metric tonnes. Heavier cargo falls back to Interstellar Express
automatically and the rate difference is refunded.

:::

## Pricing

Quantum Express costs 3.2 Galactic Credits per kilogram, with volume discounts from 500 kg per
cycle. See [Billing & Credits](/billing/galactic-credits) for details.
